About Research Publications Experience

01 About

I am an assistant professor at the Yau Mathematical Sciences Center (YMSC), Tsinghua University. I work in birational geometry, and I am especially interested in boundedness problems in high-dimensional algebraic geometry.

02 Research

My research concerns the minimal model program and singularity theory, with a focus on boundedness — whether the objects we study form limited families in each dimension.

  • Birational geometry
  • Minimal Model Program
  • Singularity theory
  • Boundedness in high-dimensional algebraic geometry
